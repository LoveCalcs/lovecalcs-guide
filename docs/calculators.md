# The calculators

LoveCalcs has three calculators. Each one compares two people and returns a score out of 100 with a breakdown underneath. All three are just for fun: numerology and astrology have no scientific support.

## Name love calculator

**You enter:** your name and their name.

**You get:** a love percentage and a visual breakdown across five factors:

- **Initial similarity:** how the two first letters compare.
- **Letter frequency:** which letters the names share and how often they appear.
- **Phonetic harmony:** how the names sound side by side.
- **Numerology:** a number worked out from each name and compared.
- **Vowel balance:** the rhythm and flow of the vowels in each name.

The result is calculated from the names, not drawn at random, so the same two names always give the same percentage. That makes it easy to check a friend's result or compare several pairs.

There is also an easter egg: more than 100 well-known pairs, including celebrity couples and famous memes, trigger special scores. The page also shows a Daily Love Insight that changes every day, with a short thought on relationships, a love fact and a quote.

[Open the name love calculator](https://www.lovecalcs.com/love-calculator)

## Zodiac compatibility calculator

**You enter:** two names and two star signs, picked from a list of all twelve, Aries to Pisces.

**You get:** a compatibility score built from five traditional astrological factors:

- **Sign harmony:** the traditional pairing of the two signs.
- **Elements:** fire, earth, air or water, and how they mix.
- **Modalities:** cardinal, fixed or mutable.
- **Ruling planets:** how the planets that rule each sign relate.
- **Aspect:** the distance between the signs on the zodiac wheel, such as a trine, sextile, square or opposition.

You only need to know your star signs, not exact birth times.

[Open the zodiac compatibility calculator](https://www.lovecalcs.com/zodiac-compatibility-calculator)

## Birthday compatibility calculator

**You enter:** two names and two dates of birth.

**You get:** a score that combines five areas:

- **Age gap:** closer ages score higher.
- **Seasons:** the time of year each person was born.
- **Numerology:** each person's life path number, worked out from the date.
- **Birth day patterns:** how the days of the month line up.
- **Generation:** the era each birth year falls in.

The result also includes short notes on life stages, seasons and each person's life path number.

[Open the birthday compatibility calculator](https://www.lovecalcs.com/date-of-birth-calculator)

## Reading a calculator score

A high score is not a promise and a low one is not a warning. The fun is in comparing results and seeing which parts of the breakdown you agree with. Try all three calculators for the same couple: they use completely different inputs, so it is common to get a high name score and a middling zodiac score, and that difference is often more entertaining than any single number.
