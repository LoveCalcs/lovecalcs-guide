# LoveCalcs Guide

[LoveCalcs](https://www.lovecalcs.com) is a free website of love calculators and relationship quizzes. This guide describes each tool in plain language: what you type in, what comes back, and how to get the most out of the result.

## At a glance

- **Price:** nothing. Every tool is free.
- **Account:** none needed. You open a page and start.
- **Languages:** 11 in total: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic and Hindi.
- **Purpose:** entertainment and conversation. None of the results are professional or relationship advice.

## The two kinds of tool

**Calculators** take a small piece of information about two people, such as names, star signs or dates of birth, and return a compatibility score with a breakdown. They are games built on numerology, astrology and wordplay. See [The calculators](calculators.md).

**Quizzes** ask you a short set of multiple-choice questions about your own habits and preferences, then describe your style. You answer them alone and compare results with a partner afterwards. See [The quizzes](quizzes.md).

## A suggested first visit

1. Start with the [name love calculator](https://www.lovecalcs.com/love-calculator), the quickest tool on the site.
2. If you know both star signs, try the [zodiac compatibility calculator](https://www.lovecalcs.com/zodiac-compatibility-calculator) next.
3. For something more practical, each take the [love language quiz](https://www.lovecalcs.com/love-language-assessment) and compare answers.

## Ways to use it

Couples can use the quizzes as a light way to start a conversation they might otherwise put off, such as how they each handle an argument or where they want to be in five years. Friends can use the calculators as a party game, testing every possible pairing in the room. Content creators can use the shareable result images from the name calculator. Or you can simply type your own name next to a crush's and see what comes out.

## Sharing your results

The name love calculator can turn a result into an image sized for social media, so you can post it or send it to the person you tested. Because the score for any pair of names never changes, a friend who types the same two names will see exactly what you saw.

## A note on accuracy

The calculators are for fun. Name scores, star sign matches and birthday numerology have no scientific basis, and a number on a screen cannot tell you whether two people belong together. The quizzes are a little different because they ask about your real habits, but they are still short, self-scored questionnaires rather than professional assessments. If something in your relationship is worrying you, a qualified counsellor is the right person to talk to.
