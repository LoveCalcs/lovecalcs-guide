# The quizzes

The quizzes are short sets of multiple-choice questions. Unlike the calculators, they ask about you: how you show affection, how you talk things through, what you want from the years ahead. Each ends with a result you can compare with a partner's.

| Quiz | Questions | What the result shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Love languages](https://www.lovecalcs.com/love-language-assessment) | 10 | Which of the five love languages scores highest for you, with a percentage for each |
| [Communication style](https://www.lovecalcs.com/communication-style-quiz) | 10 | The communication style your answers match, with a short description |
| [Conflict resolution](https://www.lovecalcs.com/conflict-resolution-style) | 10 | Your main approach to disagreements, plus how strongly each of five approaches shows up |

| [Relationship compatibility](https://www.lovecalcs.com/relationship-quiz) | 8 | A quick check on how your relationship feels right now |

## What the questions are like

Each question gives you four or five everyday answers to choose from. A few examples of the ground they cover:

- **Love languages** opens by asking what makes you feel most loved, with answers ranging from hearing kind words to getting a hug, having help with a task, spending time together or receiving a thoughtful gift.
- **Communication style** asks how you react when your partner is upset: listening and asking questions, offering a practical fix, giving a hug, or giving them space.
- **Conflict resolution** asks what you do first when you disagree about something important, from looking for a solution that suits you both to giving in or walking away.
- **Future goals** starts with your career plans for the next five years, such as leadership, work-life balance, running a business or working remotely while travelling.
- **Relationship compatibility** begins with how often the two of you have meaningful conversations.

There are no trick questions and no right answers. Pick the option closest to what you would really do, not what you think you should do.

## Getting the most from a quiz

- **Answer separately.** Take the quiz on your own so your partner's answers do not steer yours.
- **Guess first.** Before you swap results, try to predict what the other person got.
- **Talk about the gaps.** The places where your answers differ are usually the most interesting part.
- **Repeat later.** Habits change. Taking a quiz again in a few months can show how things have shifted.

The love languages framework is popular and handy for starting a conversation, though the research behind it is mixed. Treat every quiz result as a prompt to talk, not a verdict.
